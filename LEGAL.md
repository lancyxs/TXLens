Legal Compliance & Ownership Declaration

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

OWNERSHIP & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
The TXLens application, including its transaction parsing engine, multi-language localization system, address labeling database, token metadata integration, and professional user interface aesthetics, is the sole and exclusive property of the TXLens Development Team. This declaration serves as the official confirmation of ownership and proprietary rights for the application's distribution.

DATA PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY
We are committed to the highest standards of data security and user privacy. TXLens is designed as a standalone, read-only blockchain explorer with no account system. All user-specific data — including search history, language preferences, cached token metadata, and wallet session tokens — is stored exclusively on the user's device using local storage. The developer does not maintain, control, or have access to any centralized servers that process or store user data, and no personal information (email, phone number, device identifiers, or tracking data) is ever collected.

To retrieve publicly available blockchain data, the queries you enter (wallet addresses, transaction signatures, or domain names) are transmitted to third-party RPC providers and indexing services. No other personal data leaves your device.

NON-CUSTODIAL COMPLIANCE
Non-Custodial Exploration: TXLens is a strictly non-custodial, read-only viewing and analysis utility. When users browse and analyze on-chain data (such as Solana blockchain transactions, tokens, and protocols), the Application merely acts as a data display and parsing tool.

No Access to Funds: We do not store, collect, or have any form of access to users' crypto wallet private keys, seed phrases, or digital fund allocations. The optional wallet connection via the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter receives only the user's public wallet address for identity display, and the Application never initiates, signs, or submits blockchain transactions.

Asset Control: Users maintain 100% autonomy over their financial choices and digital assets at all times. The developer cannot recover, view, or manage user assets under any circumstances.

GLOBAL ACCESSIBILITY & MULTI-LANGUAGE PRIORITY
To ensure a seamless global user experience, TXLens officially supports comprehensive multi-language localization across eight languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Simplified Chinese. To maintain strict technical consistency, international legal compliance, and store publishing alignment, English is established as the primary (First) language for all official system interfaces and legal documentations, with the remaining languages provided as localization options of equal functional quality.

REGULATORY RESPONSIBILITY
Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of the Application, their viewing and analysis of public blockchain data, and their interactions with various digital assets comply with all local regulations, tax obligations, and international laws within their jurisdiction.

OFFICIAL DISTRIBUTION
This application is distributed officially via the Solana Mobile dApp Store. For further legal inquiries, transparency verification, or compliance documentation, please consult the official developer channels provided within the store interface.
