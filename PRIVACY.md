TXLens: Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 30, 2026

DECENTRALIZED & NON-CUSTODIAL ARCHITECTURE
TXLens is a standalone, read-only Solana blockchain explorer.

Non-Custodial Security: We NEVER access, solicit, or store your private keys or seed phrases. We are a data viewing and analysis tool, not a wallet provider. The optional wallet connection via the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter receives only your public wallet address (and a locally stored session token) to display your on-chain activity. The Application never initiates, signs, or submits blockchain transactions.

Local-Only Storage: We do not utilize centralized servers or cloud databases. All user-specific data — your search history, language preference, cached token metadata, and wallet session token — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. The Application has no account system.

DATA MINIMIZATION & "ZERO-REGULATED DATA" LEDGE
In strict compliance with global privacy standards, we operate on a Zero-Knowledge principle:

No Regulated Data Collection: We do NOT collect, access, or store: government IDs, biometric identifiers, precise geolocation data, or financial account credentials.

No PII: We do NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any data revealing protected characteristics. The Application contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Privacy-First Exploration: We only interact with public blockchain data via open RPC endpoints. The queries you enter (wallet addresses, transaction signatures, or domain names) are transmitted to these RPC providers solely to retrieve the requested public on-chain data. No other personal data is ever transmitted to or stored by us.

AGE RESTRICTION
By using TXLens, you represent that you are at least 18 years of age (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any data from individuals under 18.

USER RIGHTS & DATA DELETION
Data Control: Since your data is stored locally, "deleting an account" or "erasing data" is achieved by deleting the application or clearing the application data/storage from your device settings. Disconnecting your wallet from within the Application immediately removes the locally stored wallet session.

Opt-out: You may cease all data interaction by simply closing or uninstalling the application.

BLOCKCHAIN INTERACTION & NO-REFUND POLICY
Read-Only Design: The Application is strictly read-only. It displays and parses public blockchain data but never initiates, signs, or broadcasts transactions. Any on-chain operations you perform are conducted entirely through your own wallet, outside this Application, and are final and cannot be reversed by the developer.

No Refunds: As a free data viewing tool, we do not provide, sell, or manage digital assets; therefore, no refund claims apply to this application.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
The Application uses standard public RPC providers and indexing services (e.g., Helius, public Solana RPC endpoints), on-chain name services (e.g., Bonfida SNS, ONS), and retrieves token/NFT media from decentralized storage networks (e.g., Arweave, IPFS). These providers process requests per their own privacy policies. We do not control these third parties.

RIGHT TO DECLINE & CONSENT
By clicking "AGREE" or continuing to use TXLens, you provide explicit, non-coercive consent to these terms. If you do not agree, you must immediately exit and uninstall the application.
